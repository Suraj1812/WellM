# App screenshots

These screenshots show the app running at `http://localhost:8081` in a browser with a 430 × 932 phone viewport. They are tightly framed to the app viewport, without browser chrome. Each image retains the visible **Interactive preview · sample nights · microphone off** banner.

| Screenshot             | Visible state                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Tonight](tonight.png) | Start screen, bedside illustration, and sample preview action.                                                                                              |
| [Morning](morning.png) | Sample morning score, estimated snoring minutes, and recorded minutes.                                                                                      |
| [Week](week.png)       | Seven sample nights, counted-night average, and the selected noisy night's exclusion reason. The content is scrolled to show the chart and reason together. |

The images are direct captures of the rendered UI. Their contents were not generated, retouched, or composited. The bedside photograph and logo used by the app have separate provenance in [assets.md](../assets.md).

These browser screenshots demonstrate the interface with sample data. They do not establish microphone capture, on-device classification, playback, or 30-minute listening with a locked screen. The application requires a separate two-minute recording from a physical phone, tested with a snoring clip playing on another device. Follow [device-testing.md](../device-testing.md) for that evidence.
